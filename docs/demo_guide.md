# SIH Jury Presentation & Demo Walkthrough Guide

Use this script to deliver an unforgettable Smart India Hackathon jury presentation:

---

## 🎬 3-Minute Live Demonstration Script

### Part 1: The Core Problem & Persona (0:00 - 0:45)
1. **Open the Landing Page**: Point out the tagline *"Connecting Every Patient to the Right Care, at the Right Time."*
2. **Click "Play Meena's Healthcare Journey"** on the top demo bar:
   - Introduce **Meena Patil**, a 24-year-old pregnant mother (28 weeks) in Gandhre Village, Kalyan Rural.
   - Explain how in traditional setups, Meena would face fragmented paper cards, long bus travel, and unnoticed gestational hypertension.

### Part 2: Frontline Triage & Teleconsultation (0:45 - 1:45)
3. **Show ASHA Worker Sunita's Workstation**:
   - Show vitals recording: BP is elevated at `152/96 mmHg` with headache and swollen feet.
   - Click **Triage**: Show the rule-based engine outputting `⚠️ HIGH RISK (Pre-Eclampsia Risk)`.
   - Highlight the clinical rationale and emphasize that it is **decision support**, not replacing doctors.
   - Switch network to **Offline Mode** to demonstrate PWA field caching, then toggle **Online** and click **Sync Now**.
4. **Launch Teleconsultation with Doctor Dr. Rajesh Kulkarni**:
   - Demonstrate the **Low-Bandwidth Mode** toggle (essential for rural 2G/3G connectivity).
   - Show doctor reviewing longitudinal EHR charts (Blood Pressure progression over time).
   - Doctor orders Spot Urine Protein and generates electronic Rx for Tab Labetalol 100mg.

### Part 3: Public Lab & 8-Stage Referral (1:45 - 2:30)
5. **Switch to Diagnostic Lab Technician Prakash**:
   - Technician views specimen request and digitally verifies `Urine Albumin +2 (Proteinuria)`.
6. **Switch to Patient Meena & Doctor Referral**:
   - Doctor initiates high-priority electronic referral to **Thane District Civil Hospital OB/GYN Unit**.
   - Show the **Referral Tracker Stepper** transitioning from `Created → Sent → Accepted by Specialist Dr. Ananya Joshi`.

### Part 4: Government District Dashboard & Value Proposition (2:30 - 3:00)
7. **Switch to District Health Officer Dr. Sandeep Mane**:
   - Show the **Maharashtra GIS Map** with live facility occupancy, wait times, and medicine alerts.
   - Show referral completion rate (88%), reduced average waiting times (24 mins), and zero lost medical records.
   - Conclude: **"SWASTHYA SETU does not replace government infrastructure — it empowers it with an intelligent care-access and continuity layer."**
